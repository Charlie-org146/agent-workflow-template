## Purpose

A goal's plan is ready in a set time and never stalls on a tool fault.

## Done when

- Expected change: goals stopped by a planning fault go from 2 to 0, and a bug fix plans in 10 minutes or less.
- A bug-fix goal whose plan step runs past 10 minutes is reported over its limit, and the board row reads "missed".
- Freeze a bug-fix goal. Its plan record shows exactly one review. A feature goal's record shows the full set.
- Three stand-in reviews of 2, 4 and 6 minutes produce a ready plan in under 8 minutes.
- A stand-in guard refuses with "evidence". The goal goes on through the fallback, its record quotes the refusal, and one issue is opened.
- After a plan finishes, the board shows each review's minutes and tokens. A review recorded at three times its usual shows a flag.

# Check-in and board

Milestone: https://github.com/jcl2018/agent-workflow-template/milestone/6

Review: **Unreviewed** <!-- kept merge-queue-ci@2b2c3a54a -->

How to use: [How-to-use-Check-in-and-board](How-to-use-Check-in-and-board)

## Problem

- I can see what is running, stuck or waiting on me, the same way every time, without opening each session.

## Requirements

### Functional

- CHK-1: **Unreviewed** One live board and one check-in layout show where everything stands
  - **The board** has these sections, in order, each collapsible: Your move (only my items); Red team (bottlenecks now, planning suggestions with taken or why not, the biggest risk, a verdict per principle); Release goals (short: one line, one bar, an ETA, at most five steps, each step with its own progress bar and ETA, and whose ETA it is); Merge queue (a card per pull request in columns: Building, PR checks, In the queue, Merged today, Held, read from GitHub); Task queue (every open task by expected time on main, with a state and whose ETA it is); Workers; Shipped. [CHK-1]
  - **Every item is a card;** no tables and no plain lists. [CHK-1]
  - **The check-in** follows the same order and opens with what slipped; a hook blocks a check-in reply in any other layout. [CHK-1]
  - **The check-in shows each repo as a queue:** "Building now" (each running goal with its PR, session, step, progress and ETA), then "Queue, in order" (position, goal, what it waits for, ETA to start); a goal is in one block only, and every row shows an ETA or "ETA unknown: <why>". [CHK-1]
  - **Under each release, each goal shows a progress bar per frozen done line** (done/total from the regression rows linked to that line, else the goal's flow steps), each with an ETA, and the release shows a roll-up bar with its ETA. [CHK-1]
  - Text is plain and reads on a phone. [CHK-1]
  - Every claim carries what it was read from and its clock time. [CHK-1]
  - One email stream, which I answer by reply. [CHK-1]

### Non-functional

None

## Non-goals

- The check-in and board only report: they never start, stop or merge a goal; the operation session does (Operation).
- They never show merged work or history, and never ask me another machine's questions.

## Success metrics

- The board and every full check-in print in the same layout every time, at least every 30 minutes by default.
- Every item under Needs you says what it holds up, and the board builds in under 10 seconds.
